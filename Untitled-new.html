Kashi Experiences

Discover the Many Sides of Varanasi

Temples & Pilgrimage

Explore Kashi Vishwanath, Kaal Bhairav, Mahamritunjay Mahadev and other sacred places of Kashi.

Ghats & Ganga Aarti

Experience the spiritual atmosphere of Varanasi's sacred ghats and witness the mesmerizing evening Ganga Aarti.

Heritage & Sightseeing

Discover Sarnath, BHU, Manikarnika and the historic character of Kashi.

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

Visit one of India's most revered Shiva temples and a central spiritual landmark of Kashi.

Kaal Bhairav Temple

Explore the ancient temple dedicated to Kaal Bhairav, traditionally regarded as the guardian deity of Kashi.

Dashashwamedh Ghat

Discover one of Varanasi's most iconic riverfront ghats and a major venue for the spectacular Ganga Aarti.

Assi Ghat

Enjoy peaceful mornings, riverside walks and cultural experiences at one of the southernmost major ghats of Varanasi.

Manikarnika Ghat

Visit one of Varanasi's oldest and most significant ghats, deeply connected with the city's spiritual traditions.

Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple

A revered Hanuman temple known for its peaceful spiritual atmosphere and long-standing religious traditions.

Durga Temple

Admire the distinctive red architecture of this historic temple dedicated to Goddess Durga.

Mahamritunjay Mahadev Temple

Seek blessings at this sacred Shiva temple, known for its spiritual significance among devotees.

Kedar Ghat

Experience the devotional atmosphere and colorful riverside setting of this historic ghat.

Tulsi Ghat

Explore a historic riverside ghat associated with poet-saint Tulsidas and the cultural heritage of Kashi.

Ramnagar Fort

Discover the historic riverside fort and its royal heritage, museum collections and traditional architecture.

Sarnath

Visit the historic Buddhist site where Gautama Buddha is traditionally said to have delivered his first sermon.

Dhamek Stupa

See one of Sarnath's most prominent ancient monuments and an important site in Buddhist history.

Banaras Hindu University

Explore the historic campus of one of India's renowned universities and visit the beautiful New Vishwanath Temple.

New Vishwanath Temple

Admire the elegant white marble temple located within the Banaras Hindu University campus.

Bharat Mata Temple

Discover the unique temple featuring a marble relief map representing the Indian subcontinent.

Nepali Temple

Admire the distinctive wooden architecture and peaceful setting of this historic temple near Lalita Ghat.

Ahilyabai Ghat

Experience the heritage and serene riverside views of this historic ghat along the Ganges.

Ganga Boat Ride

Enjoy a memorable boat ride along the Ganges and experience the timeless beauty of Varanasi from the river.

Varanasi International Cricket Stadium

Discover Varanasi's modern sporting landmark and an important new addition to the city's attractions.
